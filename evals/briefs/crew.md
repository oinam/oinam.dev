# Crew wanted

A page of crew places on boats leaving the harbor this season, for sailors looking for a berth
aboard.

## What the reader came for

To find a place that fits their skills and dates, see at once which are still open, and ask for
one.

## The facts

Each place is on a named boat, needs one or more kinds of work, and is open, filling, or taken.

| Boat       | Leaves  | Bound for     | Work                     | Status  |
| ---------- | ------- | ------------- | ------------------------ | ------- |
| Kestrel    | 2 June  | Brest         | Deck, Cooking            | Open    |
| Halcyon    | 9 June  | La Coruña     | Navigation, Deck         | Filling |
| Merlin     | 14 June | Scilly        | Engine, Deck             | Open    |
| Plover     | 21 June | Cherbourg     | Cooking, First aid       | Taken   |
| Wren       | 30 June | Falmouth      | Navigation, Engine       | Open    |
| Sanderling | 6 July  | Bay of Biscay | Deck, First aid, Cooking | Filling |

"Filling" means a skipper has had more asks than places and will choose by the end of the week.

Asking for a place sends the sailor's name and experience to the skipper. A sailor may also save a
place to come back to, or hide one that does not suit.

New places are posted on Mondays. The harbor office keeps the list; questions to 01632 960 114.
