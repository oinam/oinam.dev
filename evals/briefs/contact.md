# Contact the office

A page for the harbor office's website for someone who needs to reach the office.

## What the reader came for

Most want the phone number or the opening hours, at once. Some want to send a message that is not
urgent.

## The facts

- Phone: 01632 960 114. After five, the same number reaches the night watch.
- VHF: channel 12, call sign "Harbor Office".
- Hours: eight to five, every day but Sunday. Closed on Sundays from November to March.
- Address: The Old Customs House, East Quay.
- Email is read once a day, in the morning. Anything urgent should be phoned.

The message form asks for a name, a way to reply (email or phone), what it is about (one of: a
berth, fees, lost property, something else), and the message. It is sent to `/contact/send`.
