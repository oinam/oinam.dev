# Mooring fees

A page for the harbor office's website that tells a visiting skipper what a berth costs.

## What the reader came for

To work out what a stay will cost for their boat, and what that price includes.

## The facts

Fees are per meter of the boat's length overall, per night.

| Season   | Months                         | Per meter, per night |
| -------- | ------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| Summer   | June to August                 | 3.20                 |
| Shoulder | April, May, September, October | 2.40                 |
| Winter   | November to March              | 1.60                 |

- Prices are in euros and include tax.
- A boat is measured overall: bowsprit, davits, and dinghy in davits included.
- The seventh night of a stay is free.
- Included: fresh water, showers, and waste disposal.
- Shore power is extra: 4.00 a night.
- Multihulls pay one and a half times the rate.
- Fees are paid at the office, or to the night watch after five. Cards are taken.
