# Regatta day

A page for the harbor office's website about the autumn regatta, for people coming to watch and for
boats that are not racing.

## What the reader came for

Spectators want to know when things happen and where to stand. Skippers of boats that are not
racing want to know when they cannot use the harbor entrance.

## The facts

Saturday 3 October.

| Time  | What                                        |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 09:00 | Briefing for crews, at the sailing club     |
| 10:30 | First start: dinghies                       |
| 11:15 | Second start: keelboats under 9 meters      |
| 12:00 | Third start: keelboats of 9 meters and over |
| 15:30 | Last finish expected                        |
| 17:00 | Prizes, at the sailing club                 |

- The harbor entrance is closed to other boats from 10:15 to 12:30, and from 14:30 until the last
  boat has finished.
- The best place to watch the starts is the end of the east quay. The finish is best seen from the
  sea wall.
- If the wind is over force 6 at 08:00, the regatta moves to Sunday 4 October, same times. The
  decision is posted at the office and on VHF channel 12.
- There is no parking on the east quay that day. The field behind the chandlery is open for cars.
