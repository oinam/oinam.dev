# Search results

The results page of the harbor office's website search.

## What the reader came for

To find the page that answers their question, quickly, or to learn that there is none and what to
do then.

## The facts

Build the page for the search "shore power", which has these four results, in this order:

1. Mooring fees, `/fees/`. "Shore power is extra: 4.00 a night."
2. Book a berth, `/book/`. "Say whether you want shore power when you book."
3. Berths, `/berths/`. "Every second berth on the east quay has shore power."
4. Notice: the harbor office moves, `/notices/office-move/`. "Shore power cards are collected
   from the new office."

The page has the search field at the top, holding the words searched for.

Also build, on the same page under a clear heading so both can be compared, what the page shows
for a search with no results, "anchor chain". The office's advice then is to phone, on 01632 960
114, or to ask at the chandlery.
