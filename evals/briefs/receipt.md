# A receipt for a stay

A page a skipper sees, and may print, after paying for a stay.

## What the reader came for

To check what they were charged and why, and to keep a record.

## The facts

Receipt 2026-0418, paid on 14 August by card.

- Boat: Kestrel, 9.2 meters overall.
- Berth: E 11.
- Arrived 10 August, left 14 August: four nights.

| Line                             | Sum    |
