Colors, number 29

Brown

A warm brown.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(24.5% 0.018 48deg) #281e19 oklch(91.7% 0.036 75.5deg) #f2e1ca Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(98.4% 0.005 67.8deg) #fcf9f6 oklch(17.8% 0.004 84.6deg) #12110f The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.5% 0.002 67.8deg) #fefdfc oklch(21.3% 0.007 48.4deg) #1c1816 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(48.1% 0.014 50.3deg) #655c57 oklch(68.1% 0.026 76deg) #a29787 Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(55.5% 0.012 51.3deg) #79716d oklch(60.7% 0.023 76.2deg) #8a8174 Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(80.7% 0.008 57.3deg) #c4bfbb oklch(35.5% 0.012 77.9deg) #3f3b35 The edges of fields and outlined buttons.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.5 16.0 14.7 13.8
Soft 6.2 6.4 6.6 6.1
Faint 4.6 4.7 4.9 4.6

The accent

The one main action of a group may be an accent button, the solid button in color. This color takes it from its ink: the same hue, with enough color to be seen, and as light by day, or as deep by night, as it can be while its label reads at 7 to 1. Its fill stands at 3 to 1 or better against the page and a raised surface.

Token Light Dark What it is for
--color-accent oklch(46.5% 0.096 75.5deg) #785004 oklch(70.5% 0.148 75.5deg) #d49102 The fill: 6.8 to 1 against the page by day, 6.6 by night.
--color-accent-text oklch(100% 0 0deg) #ffffff oklch(17.8% 0.004 84.6deg) #12110f The label: 7.1 to 1 on the fill by day, 7.1 by night.

Where it comes from

The brown scale of Radix Colors, under the MIT license: its step 12 is the ink, and its steps 2 and 1 are the cloud and the milk; the same steps of its dark scale make the night side. The ink is deeper here than there by day, with the same hue and chroma, so that the faint text reaches 4.5 to 1 when it is mixed the way these components mix it.

Use it

Take a copy of 29.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/brown.css" />
