Colors, number 5

Clay

A dark earth red on a warm, faintly pink cloud.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(23% 0.059 40.3deg) #331105 oklch(93.9% 0.024 40.3deg) #fae6df Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(97% 0.016 42.4deg) #fff2ed oklch(19% 0.03 41.3deg) #1f0f09 The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.1% 0.005 48.7deg) #fffbf9 oklch(23% 0.03 40.2deg) #291812 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Shade Token Ink in it Light Dark What it is for
Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(46.7% 0.045 40.5deg) #705247 oklch(69.9% 0.026 40.7deg) #ad9992 Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(54.1% 0.041 40.6deg) #84675d oklch(62.4% 0.027 40.8deg) #96837b Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(79.2% 0.026 41.3deg) #cbb6af oklch(37% 0.029 41.1deg) #4d3b34 The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% oklch(88.1% 0.021 41.7deg) #e5d4cd oklch(28% 0.029 41.2deg) #36241e Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.
Wash --color-wash 6% oklch(92.6% 0.019 42deg) #f2e3dd oklch(23.5% 0.03 41.3deg) #2a1913 A tint behind code, and under a row as the pointer passes.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.7 16.7 15.4 14.1
Soft 6.4 6.8 6.9 6.3
Faint 4.7 5.0 5.2 4.7

Use it

Take a copy of 5.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/clay.css" />
