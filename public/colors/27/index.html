Colors, number 27

Green

A clear green.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(23.5% 0.048 164.1deg) #022518 oklch(90.5% 0.083 158.2deg) #b1f1cb Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(98.1% 0.01 155.1deg) #f4fbf6 oklch(18.7% 0.012 167deg) #0e1512 The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.4% 0.004 157.2deg) #fbfefc oklch(21.2% 0.015 165.9deg) #121b17 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(47.4% 0.036 163.3deg) #4a6256 oklch(67.5% 0.06 158.8deg) #77a389 Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(54.8% 0.032 162.9deg) #61776c oklch(60.3% 0.053 159deg) #668b76 Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(80.2% 0.019 160.5deg) #b4c2ba oklch(35.9% 0.029 161deg) #304238 The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% oklch(89.1% 0.015 158.7deg) #d4ded8 oklch(27.3% 0.02 162.7deg) #1e2a24 Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.
Wash --color-wash 6% oklch(93.6% 0.012 157.2deg) #e4ece7 oklch(23% 0.016 164.3deg) #161f1b A tint behind code, and under a row as the pointer passes.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.6 16.2 14.4 13.7
Soft 6.3 6.5 6.5 6.2
Faint 4.6 4.7 4.9 4.6

The accent

The one main action of a group may be an accent button, the solid button in color. This color takes it from its ink: the same hue, with enough color to be seen, and as light by day, or as deep by night, as it can be while its label reads at 7 to 1. Its fill stands at 3 to 1 or better against the page and a raised surface.

Token Light Dark What it is for
--color-accent oklch(45% 0.104 158.2deg) #02663e oklch(68.5% 0.15 158.2deg) #25b576 The fill: 6.7 to 1 against the page by day, 6.7 by night.
--color-accent-text oklch(100% 0 0deg) #ffffff oklch(18.7% 0.012 167deg) #0e1512 The label: 7.1 to 1 on the fill by day, 7.0 by night.

Where it comes from

The green scale of Radix Colors, under the MIT license: its step 12 is the ink, and its steps 2 and 1 are the cloud and the milk; the same steps of its dark scale make the night side. The ink is deeper here than there by day, with the same hue and chroma, so that the faint text reaches 4.5 to 1 when it is mixed the way these components mix it.

Use it

Take a copy of 27.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/green.css" />
