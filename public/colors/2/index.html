Colors, number 2

Navy

A bright navy for text by day, and a deep one for the ground by night, on a cloud of the same blue.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(24% 0.101 264.4deg) #06194e oklch(94.2% 0.016 248deg) #e4edf6 Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(97.5% 0.007 260.7deg) #f4f7fc oklch(21.4% 0.063 265.5deg) #0b1736 The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.4% 0.003 264.5deg) #fcfdff oklch(24.6% 0.07 266.4deg) #111e42 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Shade Token Ink in it Light Dark What it is for
Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(47.5% 0.071 264.3deg) #485c85 oklch(70.9% 0.031 259.4deg) #96a2b5 Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(54.9% 0.062 264.2deg) #5f7196 oklch(63.6% 0.035 261deg) #7f8ca1 Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(79.9% 0.03 263.7deg) #b4bed1 oklch(38.9% 0.052 264.2deg) #374461 The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% oklch(88.7% 0.018 263.2deg) #d3dae6 oklch(30.1% 0.057 264.9deg) #202d4b Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.
Wash --color-wash 6% oklch(93.1% 0.013 262.5deg) #e4e8f1 oklch(25.8% 0.06 265.2deg) #152240 A tint behind code, and under a row as the pointer passes.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.6 16.4 14.9 13.8
Soft 6.2 6.6 6.8 6.3
Faint 4.6 4.8 5.2 4.8

The accent

The one main action of a group may be an accent button, the solid button in color. This color takes it from its ink: the same hue, with enough color to be seen, and as light by day, or as deep by night, as it can be while its label reads at 7 to 1. Its fill stands at 3 to 1 or better against the page and a raised surface.

Token Light Dark What it is for
--color-accent oklch(46.5% 0.15 264.4deg) #2d52ac oklch(72.5% 0.14 264.4deg) #7aa3fe The fill: 6.7 to 1 against the page by day, 6.6 by night.
--color-accent-text oklch(100% 0 0deg) #ffffff oklch(21.4% 0.063 265.5deg) #0b1736 The label: 7.2 to 1 on the fill by day, 7.1 by night.

Use it

Take a copy of 2.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/navy.css" />
