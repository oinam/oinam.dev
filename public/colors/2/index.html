Colors, number 2

Paper

Soft black on warm paper, like a printed page.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(21.8% 0.006 91.6deg) #1b1a17 oklch(95.3% 0.01 87.5deg) #f2efe8 Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(96.8% 0.009 84.6deg) #f7f4ee oklch(17.8% 0.004 84.6deg) #12110f The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.1% 0.004 91.4deg) #fdfcf9 oklch(21.9% 0.007 78.2deg) #1c1a17 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Shade Token Ink in it Light Dark What it is for
Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(45.8% 0.007 88.7deg) #595753 oklch(70.5% 0.008 87deg) #a2a09a Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(53.3% 0.007 88deg) #6e6c68 oklch(62.7% 0.007 86.8deg) #8a8883 Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(78.8% 0.008 85.8deg) #bcbab4 oklch(36.4% 0.005 85.9deg) #3f3e3b The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% oklch(87.8% 0.009 85.2deg) #d9d6d1 oklch(27.1% 0.005 85.3deg) #282624 Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.
Wash --color-wash 6% oklch(92.3% 0.009 84.9deg) #e8e5df oklch(22.4% 0.004 85deg) #1d1b19 A tint behind code, and under a row as the pointer passes.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.9 17.0 16.4 15.1
Soft 6.6 7.0 7.2 6.6
Faint 4.8 5.1 5.3 4.9

Use it

Take a copy of 2.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/paper.css" />
