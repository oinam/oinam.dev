Colors, number 24

Cyan

A bright blue-green.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(23.5% 0.045 225.9deg) #00222e oklch(91% 0.057 211.5deg) #b6ecf7 Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(97.9% 0.009 205.9deg) #f2fafb oklch(19.2% 0.018 222.8deg) #0b161a The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.2% 0.003 219.5deg) #fafdfe oklch(21.4% 0.019 227.9deg) #101b20 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(47.3% 0.033 224.2deg) #47606a oklch(68% 0.044 213deg) #79a0a9 Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(54.7% 0.03 223.4deg) #5f757e oklch(60.8% 0.041 213.6deg) #678991 Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(80% 0.017 218.2deg) #b2c1c5 oklch(36.4% 0.027 217.1deg) #2e4248 The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% oklch(89% 0.013 214deg) #d1dde0 oklch(27.8% 0.023 219.4deg) #1c2b30 Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.
Wash --color-wash 6% oklch(93.4% 0.011 210.7deg) #e1ebed oklch(23.5% 0.02 220.9deg) #132025 A tint behind code, and under a row as the pointer passes.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.6 16.2 14.3 13.6
Soft 6.3 6.5 6.5 6.2
Faint 4.6 4.7 4.9 4.6

The accent

The one main action of a group may be an accent button, the solid button in color. This color takes it from its ink: the same hue, with enough color to be seen, and as light by day, or as deep by night, as it can be while its label reads at 7 to 1. Its fill stands at 3 to 1 or better against the page and a raised surface.

Token Light Dark What it is for
--color-accent oklch(45.5% 0.078 211.5deg) #04626f oklch(69.5% 0.12 211.5deg) #07b0c7 The fill: 6.7 to 1 against the page by day, 6.7 by night.
--color-accent-text oklch(100% 0 0deg) #ffffff oklch(19.2% 0.018 222.8deg) #0b161a The label: 7.0 to 1 on the fill by day, 7.0 by night.

Where it comes from

The cyan scale of Radix Colors, under the MIT license: its step 12 is the ink, and its steps 2 and 1 are the cloud and the milk; the same steps of its dark scale make the night side. The ink is deeper here than there by day, with the same hue and chroma, so that the faint text reaches 4.5 to 1 when it is mixed the way these components mix it.

Use it

Take a copy of 24.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/cyan.css" />
