Colors, number 12

Sand

A warm grey, like dry sand.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(24.3% 0.008 95.4deg) #21201c oklch(94.9% 0.003 106.5deg) #eeeeec Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(98.2% 0.001 106.4deg) #f9f9f8 oklch(17.7% 0.002 106.6deg) #111110 The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.4% 0.001 106.4deg) #fdfdfc oklch(21.3% 0.002 106.6deg) #191918 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Shade Token Ink in it Light Dark What it is for
Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(47.9% 0.006 96deg) #5e5d5a oklch(70.2% 0.003 106.5deg) #9f9f9d Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(55.3% 0.005 96.3deg) #73726f oklch(62.5% 0.003 106.5deg) #888886 Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(80.5% 0.003 98.5deg) #bfbfbd oklch(36.2% 0.002 106.6deg) #3e3e3c The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% oklch(89.3% 0.002 100.7deg) #dcdcda oklch(27% 0.002 106.6deg) #262625 Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.
Wash --color-wash 6% oklch(93.8% 0.001 102.7deg) #eaeae9 oklch(22.3% 0.002 106.6deg) #1b1b1a A tint behind code, and under a row as the pointer passes.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.5 16.0 16.3 15.1
Soft 6.3 6.5 7.1 6.6
Faint 4.6 4.7 5.3 5.0

The accent

The one main action of a group may be an accent button, the solid button in color. This color's inks are grey, with no color to lend, so its accent is its ink, as the solid button is. Its fill stands at 3 to 1 or better against the page and a raised surface.

Token Light Dark What it is for
--color-accent oklch(24.3% 0.008 95.4deg) #21201c oklch(94.9% 0.003 106.5deg) #eeeeec The fill: 15.5 to 1 against the page by day, 15.1 by night.
--color-accent-text oklch(98.2% 0.001 106.4deg) #f9f9f8 oklch(17.7% 0.002 106.6deg) #111110 The label: 15.5 to 1 on the fill by day, 16.3 by night.

Where it comes from

The sand scale of Radix Colors, under the MIT license: its step 12 is the ink, and its steps 2 and 1 are the cloud and the milk; the same steps of its dark scale make the night side. The values are as they are there.

Use it

Take a copy of 12.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/sand.css" />
