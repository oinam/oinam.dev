Colors

Pairs of colors for the components. Each is three tokens, and every other shade is mixed from them, in light and in dark.

A color has a number, which is its address, and a name, which is only for reading: /colors/1/navy/ and /colors/1/ are the same page. So a color can be renamed, and every link to it still works. The same list is in colors.json, as data. Every text shade in every color reaches 4.5 to 1, in both themes; a test holds them there.

Colors are written in OKLCH: lightness, chroma, and hue, in that order. Two colors with the same hue are the same color at another depth, and it shows in the numbers. Each color's own page has its values, and the same colors in hex for a tool that wants them. Here, each row is a color by day and by night, from the page to the text: cloud, milk, the two lines, and the three inks. The wash is left out here, since it is not a color of its own but a faint tint of ink over whatever is under it; each color's page has it.
