Colors, number 40

Rose

A soft red leaning pink.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(24.8% 0.099 14.1deg) #450012 oklch(96.9% 0.015 12.4deg) #fff1f2 Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(96.9% 0.015 12.4deg) #fff1f2 oklch(27% 0.105 12.3deg) #4d0218 The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(100% 0 0deg) #ffffff oklch(31.2% 0.105 12.2deg) #5a1022 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Shade Token Ink in it Light Dark What it is for
Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(47.9% 0.072 14deg) #814c51 oklch(74.5% 0.044 12.3deg) #c7a2a5 Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(55.1% 0.064 13.9deg) #936266 oklch(67.5% 0.053 12.3deg) #b58a8e Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(79.6% 0.035 13.5deg) #d2b4b6 oklch(43.8% 0.083 12.3deg) #793d45 The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% oklch(88.2% 0.025 13.2deg) #e8d2d3 oklch(35.4% 0.094 12.3deg) #63222e Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.
Wash --color-wash 6% oklch(92.6% 0.02 12.9deg) #f4e1e3 oklch(31.2% 0.1 12.3deg) #581323 A tint behind code, and under a row as the pointer passes.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.2 16.6 14.3 12.5
Soft 6.2 6.8 6.8 6.0
Faint 4.6 5.0 5.2 4.6

The accent

The one main action of a group may be an accent button, the solid button in color. This color takes it from its ink: the same hue, with enough color to be seen, and as light by day, or as deep by night, as it can be while its label reads at 7 to 1. Its fill stands at 3 to 1 or better against the page and a raised surface.

Token Light Dark What it is for
--color-accent oklch(48% 0.15 14.1deg) #a02d43 oklch(77% 0.136 14.1deg) #fe8e9a The fill: 6.5 to 1 against the page by day, 6.2 by night.
--color-accent-text oklch(100% 0 0deg) #ffffff oklch(27% 0.105 12.3deg) #4d0218 The label: 7.1 to 1 on the fill by day, 7.1 by night.

Where it comes from

The rose scale of Tailwind CSS, under the MIT license: its 950 is the ink and its 50 the cloud by day, and the other way round by night; the milk is white by day, and a step lighter than the ground by night. The ink is deeper here than there by day, with the same hue and chroma, so that the faint text reaches 4.5 to 1 when it is mixed the way these components mix it.

Use it

Take a copy of 40.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/rose.css" />
