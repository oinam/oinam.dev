Colors, number 21

Iris

Between violet and blue.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(24.5% 0.099 277.7deg) #18164e oklch(91.4% 0.042 287deg) #e0dffe Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(98.1% 0.009 286.2deg) #f8f8ff oklch(19.2% 0.022 284.1deg) #13131e The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.5% 0.003 286.4deg) #fdfdff oklch(20.9% 0.029 286.6deg) #171625 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(48.1% 0.07 278deg) #545a85 oklch(68.3% 0.036 286.4deg) #9796af Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(55.4% 0.061 278.2deg) #6a6f97 oklch(61.1% 0.034 286.2deg) #818197 Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(80.4% 0.031 279.6deg) #bbbdd3 oklch(36.5% 0.027 285.2deg) #3d3d4c The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% oklch(89.3% 0.02 281.1deg) #d9dae9 oklch(27.9% 0.024 284.7deg) #272734 Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.
Wash --color-wash 6% oklch(93.7% 0.014 282.7deg) #e8e9f4 oklch(23.5% 0.023 284.4deg) #1d1d29 A tint behind code, and under a row as the pointer passes.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.7 16.4 14.2 13.7
Soft 6.2 6.5 6.4 6.2
Faint 4.6 4.8 4.8 4.7

The accent

The one main action of a group may be an accent button, the solid button in color. This color takes it from its ink: the same hue, with enough color to be seen, and as light by day, or as deep by night, as it can be while its label reads at 7 to 1. Its fill stands at 3 to 1 or better against the page and a raised surface.

Token Light Dark What it is for
--color-accent oklch(47% 0.15 277.7deg) #4a4dac oklch(71.5% 0.15 277.7deg) #8e98ff The fill: 6.7 to 1 against the page by day, 6.9 by night.
--color-accent-text oklch(100% 0 0deg) #ffffff oklch(19.2% 0.022 284.1deg) #13131e The label: 7.1 to 1 on the fill by day, 7.1 by night.

Where it comes from

The iris scale of Radix Colors, under the MIT license: its step 12 is the ink, and its steps 2 and 1 are the cloud and the milk; the same steps of its dark scale make the night side. The ink is deeper here than there by day, with the same hue and chroma, so that the faint text reaches 4.5 to 1 when it is mixed the way these components mix it.

Use it

Take a copy of 21.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/iris.css" />
