Colors, number 18

Plum

A purple leaning toward pink.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(25.5% 0.122 321deg) #3b0045 oklch(90.6% 0.055 326deg) #f4d4f4 Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(98.2% 0.01 325.7deg) #fdf7fd oklch(19% 0.018 326.2deg) #181118 The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.3% 0.004 314.8deg) #fefcff oklch(21% 0.032 326.6deg) #201320 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(48.8% 0.086 321.2deg) #764f7d oklch(67.7% 0.043 326deg) #a68fa6 Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(56% 0.075 321.3deg) #89668f oklch(60.5% 0.039 326deg) #8f7a8f Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(80.8% 0.037 322deg) #ccb9cf oklch(36.2% 0.027 326.1deg) #453945 The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% oklch(89.5% 0.023 322.8deg) #e4d7e6 oklch(27.6% 0.022 326.1deg) #2e242e Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.
Wash --color-wash 6% oklch(93.8% 0.017 323.6deg) #f0e7f1 oklch(23.3% 0.02 326.2deg) #231a23 A tint behind code, and under a row as the pointer passes.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.6 16.2 13.8 13.2
Soft 6.2 6.5 6.3 6.0
Faint 4.6 4.7 4.7 4.6

The accent

The one main action of a group may be an accent button, the solid button in color. This color takes it from its ink: the same hue, with enough color to be seen, and as light by day, or as deep by night, as it can be while its label reads at 7 to 1. Its fill stands at 3 to 1 or better against the page and a raised surface.

Token Light Dark What it is for
--color-accent oklch(48% 0.15 321deg) #823a90 oklch(72% 0.15 321deg) #cf83dd The fill: 6.7 to 1 against the page by day, 6.8 by night.
--color-accent-text oklch(100% 0 0deg) #ffffff oklch(19% 0.018 326.2deg) #181118 The label: 7.1 to 1 on the fill by day, 7.0 by night.

Where it comes from

The plum scale of Radix Colors, under the MIT license: its step 12 is the ink, and its steps 2 and 1 are the cloud and the milk; the same steps of its dark scale make the night side. The ink is deeper here than there by day, with the same hue and chroma, so that the faint text reaches 4.5 to 1 when it is mixed the way these components mix it.

Use it

Take a copy of 18.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/plum.css" />
