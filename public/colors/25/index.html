Colors, number 25

Jade

A cool green with blue in it.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(23.4% 0.041 170.2deg) #05241b oklch(90.3% 0.078 166.9deg) #adf0d4 Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(98.2% 0.009 161.4deg) #f4fbf7 oklch(18.6% 0.014 169.8deg) #0d1512 The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.4% 0.003 174.5deg) #fbfefd oklch(21.5% 0.017 168.2deg) #121c18 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Shade Token Ink in it Light Dark What it is for
Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(47.3% 0.031 169.4deg) #4b6259 oklch(67.4% 0.058 167.1deg) #74a28f Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(54.8% 0.028 169deg) #62766e oklch(60.2% 0.051 167.2deg) #638b7a Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(80.2% 0.017 166.6deg) #b5c2bc oklch(35.8% 0.029 168deg) #2d423a The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% oklch(89.2% 0.013 164.8deg) #d4ded9 oklch(27.2% 0.022 168.5deg) #1c2a25 Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.
Wash --color-wash 6% oklch(93.7% 0.011 163.4deg) #e4ede8 oklch(22.9% 0.018 169deg) #141f1b A tint behind code, and under a row as the pointer passes.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.7 16.2 14.3 13.4
Soft 6.3 6.5 6.4 6.1
Faint 4.6 4.8 4.9 4.6

Where it comes from

The jade scale of Radix Colors, under the MIT license: its step 12 is the ink, and its steps 2 and 1 are the cloud and the milk; the same steps of its dark scale make the night side. The ink is deeper here than there by day, with the same hue and chroma, so that the faint text reaches 4.5 to 1 when it is mixed the way these components mix it.

Use it

Take a copy of 25.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/jade.css" />
