Colors, number 7

Mauve

A grey with a little violet in it.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(24% 0.014 298.4deg) #201e25 oklch(95% 0.003 286.3deg) #eeeef0 Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(98.3% 0.003 308.4deg) #faf9fb oklch(18% 0.004 308.2deg) #121113 The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.2% 0.002 325.6deg) #fdfcfd oklch(21.5% 0.004 308.2deg) #1a191b Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(47.8% 0.01 299.3deg) #5e5c62 oklch(70.4% 0.003 294.7deg) #9f9fa1 Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(55.2% 0.009 299.7deg) #737177 oklch(62.7% 0.003 297.1deg) #88888a Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(80.5% 0.006 302.4deg) #c0bec2 oklch(36.5% 0.004 304.1deg) #3f3e40 The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% oklch(89.4% 0.004 304.5deg) #dcdbde oklch(27.2% 0.004 306.2deg) #272628 Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.7 16.1 16.2 15.1
Soft 6.3 6.4 7.1 6.6
Faint 4.6 4.7 5.3 5.0

The accent

The one main action of a group may be an accent button, the solid button in color. This color's inks are grey, with no color to lend, so its accent is its ink, as the solid button is. Its fill stands at 3 to 1 or better against the page and a raised surface.

Token Light Dark What it is for
--color-accent oklch(24% 0.014 298.4deg) #201e25 oklch(95% 0.003 286.3deg) #eeeef0 The fill: 15.7 to 1 against the page by day, 15.1 by night.
--color-accent-text oklch(98.3% 0.003 308.4deg) #faf9fb oklch(18% 0.004 308.2deg) #121113 The label: 15.7 to 1 on the fill by day, 16.2 by night.

Where it comes from

The mauve scale of Radix Colors, under the MIT license: its step 12 is the ink, and its steps 2 and 1 are the cloud and the milk; the same steps of its dark scale make the night side. The ink is deeper here than there by day, with the same hue and chroma, so that the faint text reaches 4.5 to 1 when it is mixed the way these components mix it.

Use it

Take a copy of 7.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/mauve.css" />
