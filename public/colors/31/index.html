Colors, number 31

Gold

A dull, antique gold.

This page wears it. Switch the theme to see the other side.

The three colors

Ink is the text, and cloud is the page. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface above it. Each is written in OKLCH, with the same color in hex under it. Press a value to copy it in OKLCH, or hold Shift as you press to copy the hex.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Ink --color-ink oklch(23.9% 0.019 77.7deg) #241e15 oklch(91.8% 0.014 78.3deg) #e9e3da Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud oklch(98.1% 0.009 100deg) #faf9f2 oklch(18.2% 0.002 106.6deg) #121211 The page.
Milk --color-milk oklch(99.4% 0.001 106.4deg) #fdfdfc oklch(21.8% 0.006 91.6deg) #1b1a17 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is ink mixed into cloud, so each one leans the way the pair leans, and each follows the theme. The components work them out; the values below are what a screen shows.

Soft --color-ink-soft 68% oklch(47.6% 0.016 81.7deg) #615c53 oklch(68.2% 0.01 80deg) #9c9892 Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% oklch(55.1% 0.015 83.3deg) #767168 oklch(60.9% 0.009 80.9deg) #86827d Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% oklch(80.3% 0.011 91.1deg) #c1beb7 oklch(35.9% 0.005 87deg) #3e3d3a The edges of fields and outlined buttons.

Contrast

Every text shade reaches WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against the page and against a raised surface, in both themes. A test holds it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Ink 15.6 16.2 14.7 13.6
Soft 6.3 6.5 6.5 6.1
Faint 4.6 4.8 4.9 4.6

The accent

The one main action of a group may be an accent button, the solid button in color. This color's inks are grey, with no color to lend, so its accent is its ink, as the solid button is. Its fill stands at 3 to 1 or better against the page and a raised surface.

Token Light Dark What it is for
--color-accent oklch(23.9% 0.019 77.7deg) #241e15 oklch(91.8% 0.014 78.3deg) #e9e3da The fill: 15.6 to 1 against the page by day, 13.6 by night.
--color-accent-text oklch(98.1% 0.009 100deg) #faf9f2 oklch(18.2% 0.002 106.6deg) #121211 The label: 15.6 to 1 on the fill by day, 14.7 by night.

Where it comes from

The gold scale of Radix Colors, under the MIT license: its step 12 is the ink, and its steps 2 and 1 are the cloud and the milk; the same steps of its dark scale make the night side. The ink is deeper here than there on both sides, with the same hue and chroma, so that the faint text reaches 4.5 to 1 when it is mixed the way these components mix it.

Use it

Take a copy of 31.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/gold.css" />
