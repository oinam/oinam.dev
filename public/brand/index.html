Brand colors

Every Oinam site wears two colors: a deep navy, and a cloud of the same blue. One more, orange, marks the brand and does nothing else.

This page wears them, as any site can, by linking one stylesheet after the components. Switch the theme to see the night side.

The two colors

Navy is the text by day and the ground by night. Cloud is the reverse. Milk is a step off cloud, for a surface that sits above the page, such as a card.

Color Token Light Dark What it is for
Navy --color-ink #0b1736 #e4edf6 Text, icons, and the solid buttons.
Cloud --color-cloud #f4f7fb #0b1736 The page.
Milk --color-milk #fcfdfe #111e42 Cards, fields, and anything raised above the page.

The shades between them

Every other shade is navy mixed into cloud, so there is no grey of its own: each one leans blue, and each follows the theme. They are worked out by the components, not chosen. The values below are what a screen shows.

Shade Token Navy in it Light Dark What it is for
Soft --color-ink-soft 68% #4b5771 #96a2b5 Running text in prose, and the lede.
Faint --color-ink-faint 58% #626d84 #7f8ca1 Hints, captions, and the quietest text.
Strong line --color-line-strong 24% #b5bcc8 #374461 The edges of fields and outlined buttons.
Line --color-line 12% #d4d9e2 #202d4b Rules, and the edges of cards and tables.
Wash --color-wash 6% #e4e8ee #152240 A tint behind code, and under a row as the pointer passes.

The mark

Two colors belong to the logo, and stay out of everything else. A page that needs to tell one thing from another does it with weight, size, or a border, as the components do.

Color Value What it is for
Oinam Blue #1d387c The logo on a light ground, and large display type in print. On a dark ground it all but vanishes, at 1.6 to 1, so there the logo is drawn in the text color.
Oinam Orange #ff6600 A mark only: a dot, a rule, a fill behind the logo. Never text on a light ground, where it reaches only 2.7 to 1. Navy on orange reads well, at 6 to 1.

Contrast

Every text color meets WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1, against both the page and a raised surface, in both themes. The garden's tests hold it there.

Text Light, on cloud Light, on milk Dark, on cloud Dark, on milk
Navy 16.4 17.3 14.9 13.8
Soft 6.7 7.1 6.8 6.3
Faint 4.8 5.1 5.2 4.8

What the old palette becomes

The palette on oinam.com had 27 colors and no roles. Each one maps to a role above, or is let go.

Old Now
Primary, Blue #1d387c Oinam Blue, for the mark. Too light to be the text: its faint shade falls to 3.4 to 1.
Secondary, Midnight #0e2532; Oxford Blue #111f46; Magpie #003658; Marine #244f81 Navy, #0b1736, which sits between Oxford Blue and Midnight.
Dark #0a0a0a; Light #fafafa Navy and cloud.
Gray-100 to Gray-900 The shades, which lean blue instead of grey.
Primary Accent #ff6600 Oinam Orange, for the mark.
Secondary Accent #ff9200; Tertiary Accent #ffbe00 Let go. One orange is a mark; three are a decoration.
Icicle, Sky, Cornflower, Pool, and Chagall Let go. Cloud carries the pale blue now.
Tertiary #7c3aed Let go. It is the violet every generated page reaches for, and it is not Oinam.

Use it

The colors are one stylesheet of three tokens, the same file as the Oinam design in the garden. Take a copy of oinam.css, keep it with the site's own styles, outside the components' folder, and link it after them: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ui/ui.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/oinam.css" />

For an agent, the rule is short: navy and cloud for everything, orange and Oinam Blue for the logo alone, and no third color in the interface.
