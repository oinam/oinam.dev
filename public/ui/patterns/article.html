Notice, 21 November

The harbor office moves to the old customs house

The move begins on Monday and takes three days. Berth bookings stay open throughout, and the phone number does not change.

What changes

From Thursday the office is on the ground floor of the old customs house, at the head of the east quay. It is two minutes on foot from where it is now, and it has the one thing the present office lacks: a door that opens onto the quay.

Keys, fuel cards, and the tide tables are collected there. The showers stay where they are, behind the chandlery.

Opening hours during the move

Day Where Hours
Monday Present office Eight to noon
Tuesday, Wednesday By phone only Eight to five
Thursday on Old customs house Eight to five
Fuel on Tuesday and Wednesday

The fuel berth is unattended on both days. Fill up on Monday, or ask the night watch.

Why now

The lease on the present office ends with the year, and the customs house has stood empty since the ferry stopped calling. Moving in winter means the fewest boats are put out by it.
