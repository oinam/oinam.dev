Patterns

Whole pages, built from the components and nothing else. The components are parts; a page is a composition, and these are compositions to follow.

Each is a page by itself, and opens with a comment in its source that says why it is laid out the way it is. The harbor office in them is made up.

The pages

Pattern What it shows
A form One narrow column, fields grouped under names, help under each field, and one button that says what it does.
A table view Many like things as a table, with one main action, a filter, a status, and each row's actions in a menu.
Settings Rows with a name on one side and a control on the other, and a dangerous action that asks first.
A list, empty and full The same list with boats expected, with none, and while it loads.
An article Writing in one column: a date, a title, a lede, plain prose, a table, and one thing not to miss.
A dashboard A sentence that says what matters now, what needs a decision, and the figures beside it. No charts.

Their own stylesheet, patterns.css, is what a project's own stylesheet looks like: short, and tokens only.
