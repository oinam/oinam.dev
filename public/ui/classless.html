Coming in at Kettle Cove

The harbor is open every day but Sunday. The channel is narrow at low water, so most boats come in on the flood and leave on the ebb. This page has what a skipper needs before the first line goes ashore.

Call on channel 12 when you round the breakwater, and wait to be given a berth.

Tides this week

Times are local. A spring tide falls on Thursday, when the low is lowest; keep to the marked channel then.

Book a berth

A berth is held until noon on the day you name. Nothing is paid until you arrive.

Over all, with the bowsprit.

Questions

Is there fuel?

Diesel at the end of the east pontoon, from eight until four.

Can I stay longer than a week?

Ask at the office. Winter berths are let from October.

Where do I leave the dinghy?

On the slip by the office, tied to the rail, never to the ladder.
