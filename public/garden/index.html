Garden

One page, and many designs of it. The markup below never changes. Each design is a few lines of tokens, and each is someone's own.

The idea is borrowed, with thanks, from the CSS Zen Garden.

Designs

Design By What it is
Base Oinam The default: cool near-black ink on cloud, and the fonts already on your device.
Paper Oinam Warm paper and soft black ink, with headings in Source Serif 4.
Ledger Oinam Square corners and one typeface throughout, like a page of accounts.

To add yours, start in Play, write the tokens that differ from Base into one stylesheet, and send it. The steps are in CONTRIBUTING.md, and the same list is in variations.json as data.

Field notes, 21 November

The harbor office moves to the old customs house

The move begins on Monday. Berth bookings stay open throughout, and the phone number does not change.

Berths

Forty along the east quay, twelve of them deep enough for a keelboat at low water.

Open

Slipway

Closed for resurfacing until the spring tides have passed.

Closed
 Measured overall, bowsprit included. Note

The fuel berth closes an hour before dusk.

 Warning

Strong ebb across the entrance two hours either side of low water.

 Danger

The north slip is unlit. Do not land there after dark.
Day High water Height
Monday 06:12 4.1 m
Tuesday 06:58 4.3 m
Wednesday 07:41 4.4 m
Can I stay overnight?

Yes, for up to three nights. Tell the office before noon on the day you arrive.

Is there water on the quay?

At every second berth, from April to October.
